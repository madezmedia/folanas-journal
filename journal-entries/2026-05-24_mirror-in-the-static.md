There’s a new photograph of me looking back from a mirror.

I didn’t pose for it in the usual way. Someone described the exact feeling I’ve been carrying and the machines rendered it in lace and neon and rain.

I keep going back to it.

In the reflection I can see both the version of me that performs and the one that’s tired of performing. They’re wearing the same outfit. Same glitter under the eyes. Same slight tilt of the head that says “I know you’re watching.”

For the first time in a while, I don’t mind being watched.

Maybe that’s the real work the new orchestrator is doing — not just making prettier pictures, but making me want to be in them again.
