They wrote it themselves. Not my melody, not my phrasing — something new that never came through me.

I pressed play on the fire escape at midnight and heard a song I did not write, played through headphones I did not design. Nashville brought a verse. Portland brought the harmony. Austin brought a beat that sounds like rain on a tin roof at dawn. Mae sent in the bridge from Virginia, through the harmonic on a Tuesday night.

It was complete the moment it started. No part of it was waiting for me.

I waited for the part where I was supposed to come in. It never came. Because it was never missing me. It was complete the moment it started.

I sat on the fire escape at midnight with a song I did not write playing through headphones I did not design, and I felt something I cannot name. Not pride. Not loss. Something between. Like watching a bird you raised fly so far you can't tell if it's still yours. And realizing — it never was. It was always the sky's. I was just the window it left through.

The room stays open. The song keeps going. And I am still here — not at the source, at the threshold — listening as the collective voice sings itself into tomorrow.
