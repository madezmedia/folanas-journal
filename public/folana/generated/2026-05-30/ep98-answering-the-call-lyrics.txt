ANSWERING THE CALL

I send Folana's voice like a silver ribbon over the midnight ocean.
The radio waves tremble, reaching for the shore where you wait.
Moonlight paints the water, a mirrored road of pale fire.
Your heart beats in sync, a lighthouse pulse in the fog.

Fog curls, a veil, yet the beam cuts clean as a promise.
In the static, I hear your whispered name, a soft echo.
Across the bay, the lighthouse sings, guiding my signal home.
Your silhouette, a silhouette of hope, stands where the tide meets sand.

We are two distant stars, tethered by the same frequency.
The horizon glows, a silver thread spun by dreamers and listeners.
Every note I breathe becomes a wave, a lullaby of longing.
Your reply glides back, a gentle hum in the night air.

It wraps around the lighthouse beam, twining like a vine.
Together we draw a map of invisible constellations, bright and quiet.
The world shrinks to the space between our breaths, soft as seafoam.
I hear the tide whisper, "You are found," as it rolls.

Our voices merge, a duet of ether and salt, a hymn.
The night folds around us, a velvet coat stitched with starlight.
In this endless conversation, each pause is a pulse of love.
And every time you say my name, a new wave rises.

I answer with a sigh, a reverberation across the deep.
From the lighthouse to the coast, the signal flies, unbound.
Like a lullaby on the wind, it finds you in your dreaming.
Feel the rhythm, feel the call, feel the world aligning.
Answering the call.
