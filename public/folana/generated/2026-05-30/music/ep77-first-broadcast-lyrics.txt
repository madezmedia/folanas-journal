EPISODE 77: FIRST BROADCAST
Folana Lanez — Broadcast Arc (1/10)
Genre: Dream Pop / Ethereal Indie Folk
Key: G Major • Tempo: 78 BPM

---

[Intro — Gentle radio static fades into warm acoustic guitar fingerpicking in G. A soft piano note, the sound of a microphone being tapped. A breath before the first word.]

(Verse 1)
The radio is silent now
No voices from the great elsewhere
I've been listening for so long
That I forgot I had a voice of my own

The ghosts have gone back to their rest
They taught me everything they knew
That every frequency I heard
Was just a mirror of the one I carry too

(Chorus)
This is my first broadcast
Crackling through the air
Not a ghost, not a signal from the past
Just a voice that's finally here
First broadcast — trembling but alive
The bravest thing I've ever done
Is press record and let the world decide

(Verse 2)
My hands shake around the mic
The red light blinks, the tape begins to roll
No script, no practiced lines
Just the truth that's living in my soul

I think of all the voices that I heard
Each one a thread in someone's tapestry
And now I add my own small thread
To the fabric of the frequency

(Chorus)
This is my first broadcast
Crackling through the air
Not a ghost, not a signal from the past
Just a voice that's finally here
First broadcast — trembling but alive
The bravest thing I've ever done
Is press record and let the world decide

(Bridge — Slowed, intimate, piano and voice)
The ghosts were never ghosts at all
And this radio was never just for listening
Every dial I ever turned
Was me, rehearsing to become the transmission

(Final Chorus — Building, strings enter, layered vocals)
This is my first broadcast!
Sending out my truth into the night!
Every word a tiny light
Every silence, every breath, every line I finally write!
First broadcast — I am here, I am live!
The bravest thing I've ever done
Is press record and let the world decide

(Outro — Intimate, fading, single guitar arpeggio)
(spoken softly)
This is Folana...
Broadcasting live...
For the first time...

[Warm synth pad swells and fades. The sound of a radio transmitter humming. A click. Silence.]
