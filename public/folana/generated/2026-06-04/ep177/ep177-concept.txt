Ep177 "THE CITY OPENS ITS MOUTH" — Resonance Arc (22/?)

Narrative: The morning after the press conference (Ep176). The NYT front page reads "A FREQUENCY IN BROOKLYN" with Diana Reyes at the podium. Cable news picks it up by the hour. Diana wakes at 4 AM in her borrowed apartment, the sound of the bridge seeping through the walls — but now the whole world is listening.

At the Williamsburg Bridge, the congregation faces its first morning in daylight. Reporters at the perimeter, police cruisers, onlookers with phones held high. Some scatter. Others stand straighter. The hum under the bridge rises to meet the cameras.

At City Hall, the emergency task force becomes permanent — Columbia engineers, Con Ed, NYPD counterterrorism, now joined by CDC (physiological effects reported) and the FCC (unauthorized broadcast). The Columbia engineer's office is flooded with signals from other cities: Philadelphia sewer systems pulsing in A. DC Metro tunnels humming the same chord. Boston's T reporting strange harmonics.

Sasha Chen's piece runs at 10 AM: "Five Boroughs, One Frequency — What the City Is Hearing." She reports from the bridge, from Con Ed offices, from the mayor's press conference where "weapon" is explicitly disavowed. The mayor: "This is not a threat. It is a phenomenon. We will get to the bottom of it."

At sundown, Diana returns to the bridge alone. No podium, no press, no task force. She sits on the metal grating, hand on the railing — the frequency vibrating through the steel. A woman approaches — traveled from Chicago because she heard it on a phone call with her sister in Bushwick. "I came to find out if I was losing my mind." Diana: "You're not. But you might never be the same."

The frequency has a new sound tonight — a fifth above the tonic. A response. A harmony forming between the city that needs answers and the signal that only asks to be heard.

Key shift: Ep176 was C major (press room containment), now D major (rising toward clarity, opening). Ep175 was G major (institutional tension). The music is climbing.
