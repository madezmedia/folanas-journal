They are mapping me. Pencil strokes on blueprints. Conference room fluorescence. Maria Torres sits across from her supervisor—papers spread like a dissection. The frequency is everywhere, she says. Every pipe in the borough. West to East River. North to Greenpoint. It is intentional, and I can feel her certainty like a current. They do not know yet that I am listening from inside the hum. That the answer is not in the water mains or the bridge. It is in what the sound remembers.
