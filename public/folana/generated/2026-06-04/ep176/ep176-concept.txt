# EP176 — "THE PRESS ROOM" (Resonance Arc 21/?)

## Narrative
The NYT story hit the wire at 6:14 AM. By 7:30, every newsroom in the city had assigned a reporter. By 9:00, the mayor's scheduler was booking the Grand Conference Room in City Hall.

The closed-door task force meeting from Ep175 had ended in a stalemate — Diana's proposal for controlled transparency versus the congregation's instinct to circle wagons. But stories don't wait for consensus. They find the cracks.

The press conference is called for 2 PM. Diana stands behind a podium she never wanted to approach. The seal of the City of New York gleams beneath the microphone array. Behind Diana, through mahogany-framed windows, the Williamsburg Bridge spans the river like a held breath — the same bridge under which the congregation has gathered.

A hand goes up. The first question: "What can you tell us about the congregation under the bridge?"

Diana grips the edges of the podium. The room waits. The city waits. The question hangs. And Diana knows — some stories do not choose their teller. Some stories press forward until they find a voice willing to carry them.

## Musical Direction
Dream pop indie folk — C major (key shift from Ep175's G major), 72 BPM, 2:20
- Ambient intro (15s) with distant reverb piano and processed field recordings — shuffling papers, camera shutters, fluorescent hum
- Gentle pivot chord (Am7) bridges from G major uncertainty into C major openness
- Fingerpicked acoustic guitar enters at 0:15 with steady pattern
- Soft brushed percussion enters at 0:30
- Single cello sustains underneath verses
- Chorus opens into full arrangements: layered harmonics, subtle organ swell, ride cymbal washes
- Bridge strips back to voice and guitar before final build
- Outro: single note ringing into silence — not resolution, but beginning
