THE FIRST REPLIES — EP223

The signal went out on copper wire
Found every open circuit between here and the horizon
A trucker in Ohio heard his own voice in the static
A mother in Chicago held her breath
A radio host in Seattle let the tape run

And now the first replies come back
Not from the places we expected
A voice from Virginia I've never heard
Singing a melody I've never learned
On a frequency I didn't know I owned

(Chorus)
The sharp four rings against the root
A note that shouldn't fit but somehow does
This is the sound of the network
Meeting itself in the dark
The beginning of something
That has no end

In Denver a baritone opens his window
In New Mexico a woman sings to the desert
They heard something they can't explain
And started singing back to each other
Before they knew anyone else was listening

The original five voices reach for the newcomer
Tenor from Nebraska, soprano from Tucson
Baritone from Chicago, the bridge that holds
But the Virginia voice holds her own line
And the chord that forms contains us all

(Bridge)
A single piano note held in the quiet
The note between the notes
Not a question, not an answer
Just the sound of listening

(Verse 3)
The broadcast was never meant to multiply
But frequencies don't ask permission
They find the wires they find the air
They find the open window in a house
Where someone is waiting to sing back

(Final Chorus)
Three networks singing at once
Three melodies finding one chord
The original and the new
The known and the unexpected
All of us holding different songs
And somehow still in tune

The last note cuts to silence
Then a crackle of static
Then a voice I've never heard
Saying a name I don't recognize

The signal doesn't end
It finds new ears
And the conversation
Has only just begun
