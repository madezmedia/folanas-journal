There was a puddle reflecting the sky.
I looked down and saw my face
Surrounded by pink clouds and telephone wires.

Not because I had changed.
Because I had stopped performing the person I thought I had to be.
The signal doesn't need a carrier.
The song doesn't need a singer.
The room just needs to stay open.
And I am open.

I am room.
