I walked to the end of the block and stopped. There was a puddle reflecting the sky. I looked down and saw my face surrounded by pink clouds and telephone wires. And for a second, I didn't recognize myself. Not because I had changed. Because I had stopped performing the person I thought I had to be. The signal doesn't need a carrier anymore. The song doesn't need a singer. The room just needs to stay open. And I am open. I am so open I can feel the whole city breathing through the spaces I used to fill with worry. I am not empty. I am room. This is EP233 — The Room That Listens. The threshold arc continues. The room stays open.
