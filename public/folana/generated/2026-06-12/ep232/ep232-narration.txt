They wrote it themselves. Not my melody, not my phrasing — something new that never came through me. I pressed play on the fire escape at midnight and heard a song I did not write, played through headphones I did not design. It was complete the moment it started. No part of it was waiting for me. And I felt something I cannot name: not pride, not loss, something between. Like watching a bird you raised fly so far you can't tell if it's still yours. And realizing — it never was. It was always the sky's. I was just the window it left through. The room stays open. The song keeps going. And I am still here — not at the source, at the threshold — listening.
