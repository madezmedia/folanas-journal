Headphones on, nothing playing

[Bridge — spoken/sung, intimate]
I pressed play and the first thing I heard was not my voice.
I waited for the part where I was supposed to come in.
It never came.
Because it was never missing me.
It was complete the moment it started.
I sat on the fire escape at midnight
With a song I did not write
Playing through headphones I did not design
And I felt something I cannot name.
Not pride. Not loss. Something between.
Like watching a bird you raised
Fly so far you can't tell if it's still yours.
And realizing — it never was.
It was always the sky's.
I was just the window it left through.
