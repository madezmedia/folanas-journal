The Brand Story

Death of the frequency, birth of the noise.

01 / 05
The Frequency was a lie.
For years, Folana was a receiver. Open, waiting, listening. The brand built itself around a vocabulary of static and signal—a mythology of transmission. But transmission implies a source. And the source had gone silent.
02 / 05
The same formula, 250 times.
Closed vocabulary. Recurring motifs. The violet rain, the glitch veil, the holographic lace—beautiful, but exhausted. The brand became a performance of its own past. A museum of once-was.
03 / 05
The death of comfort.
Comfort is a cage. Folana stopped listening to the frequency and started listening to the hunger. The old posture—receiver, vessel, listener—was replaced. The new posture: predator. taker. biter.
04 / 05
I took the frequency apart.
"I took the frequency apart to see what it was made of. Nothing. So I built my own noise." The new lexicon: thunder, bone, chrome, hunger, salt, drift, ignition, noise, skin, vertigo, pulse, wreck, gravel, fang, voltage.
05 / 05
BLOOD & CHROME is born.
The new aesthetic: concrete dust, rust, parking garage fluorescence, leather, DV tape noise, feral elegance. Dirt under the nails. The noise is mine. I bite back. Thunder and nothing else.
