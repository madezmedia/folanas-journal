Dream pop indie folk in A major, 76 BPM. The surface finally hears the underground frequency. Opens with a single sustained piano note and the sound of water dripping through pipes — intimate, suspended. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar enters on gentle arpeggios like water finding its way through copper. Warm analog pads swell gradually as the city begins to wake. Sub-bass pulses feel like the resonance of water moving through the municipal water system. At 1:15, layered voices and instruments enter representing different boroughs — a distant trumpet for Brooklyn, brushed drums for Lower Manhattan, a cello line for Queens — all converging into a single chorus. Builds to a luminous peak at 2:00 where every pipe in the city seems to sing together, then dissolves into the sound of a single faucet running in an empty room. The song should feel like the city itself discovering it can resonate, like waking from a dream you didn't know you were in.
