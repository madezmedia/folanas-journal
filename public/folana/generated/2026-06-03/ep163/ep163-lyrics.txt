[Intro]
A single piano note held in the dark,
Water dripping in the pipes beneath the city,
The city breathes in, holds, listens.

[Verse 1]
She felt it leave her hands days ago,
The river carried it downstream
But the pipes held it close —
Every basement, every wall, every faucet singing low.
A woman on the bridge stops,
Her keys fall from her hand,
She doesn't know why she's crying,
But the copper in the walls understands.

[Chorus]
The surface hears —
Not with ears, but with bone,
Every pipe a vessel, every room a throne,
The signal rises through concrete and steel,
The city wakes to something real.
The surface hears.

[Verse 2]
A truck on the BQE pulls over at dawn,
The driver kills the engine and sits,
He's chasing a song that isn't song,
A frequency the water transmits.
In Greenpoint the barista opens the back door,
The whole block exhales at once —
Every faucet running, every drain alive,
The city breathes in the resonance.

[Chorus]
The surface hears —
Not with ears, but with bone,
Every pipe a vessel, every room a throne,
The signal rises through concrete and steel,
The city wakes to something real.
The surface hears.

[Bridge]
She stands at the river's edge and feels them all,
Every fluttering heart, every sudden pause,
Every tear without a name,
Every pause without a cause.
She whispers into the current:
"I hear you. I hear you."

[Outro]
The water in the walls settles into silence,
But the silence is not empty —
It is waiting.
It has learned to listen.
The surface has woken up.
