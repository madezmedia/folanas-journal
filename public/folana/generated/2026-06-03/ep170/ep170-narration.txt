Maria Torres' report reached the city planner at 8:14 AM. By noon, a public works meeting had been called. Spectrum analyzers mapped the frequency across every pipe north of Atlantic Avenue. The needle pegged at A major and never wavered. Someone in the conference room asked the question nobody had wanted to speak aloud: "Is this intentional?" The room went silent. Because if the answer was yes — then the infrastructure was not infrastructure. It was a response. And someone — or something — had been listening all along.
