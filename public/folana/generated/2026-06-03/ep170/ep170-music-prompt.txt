Dream-pop indie-folk instrumental in A minor, 76 BPM. Opens with subtle civic field recordings — the hum of fluorescent lights in a conference room, the shuffle of papers, distant traffic. A lone sustained A drone enters like the question hanging in the air. Slow fingerpicked acoustic guitar enters hesitantly, one phrase at a time. Padded synths wash in like fluorescent hum, cold and institutional. A rhythmic pulse like scanning equipment — spectrum analyzers — builds beneath. Layers of tension accumulate as the conversation deepens. Cellos enter with long sustained notes of unease. Ends unresolved with a lone sustained chord fading into conference room silence. The feeling of a door opening to a much larger question. Production quality: warm, cinematic, indie-folk with ambient/electronic elements.
