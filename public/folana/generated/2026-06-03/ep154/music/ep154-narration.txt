My signal slipped the city's edge tonight — a tremor in the aether beyond the bridges. Towns I've never seen yet hum a frequency I only dreamed. Strangers catch it mid-stride, mid-sigh, mid-coffee — a melody they didn't know they were waiting for. It's not mine to steer. It belongs to the radio sky, to every dial left open in the dark. I'm learning to love the letting go.
