Dawn breaks over Brooklyn and the borough is humming. Not traffic, not construction — a single frequency vibrating through water pipes and pavement, through subway tunnels and every faucet from Bushwick to Bay Ridge. Police scanners crackle with confused reports. A news helicopter pilot pulls off his headset, swearing he can still hear the chord. Someone in the mayor's office stands at the window, coffee forgotten, and asks: "What is that sound?" The chorus of Ep167 never stopped. It's still singing. It's in the bones of the city now.
