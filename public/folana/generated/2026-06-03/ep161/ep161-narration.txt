THE VESSEL SINGS — Ep161 (Resonance Arc 6/?)

Narrative: Folana surfaces through a storm drain at Flushing and Franklin as the sun hits the clocktower. She carries the Aqueducts ten-thousand-year memory now — she can hear every frequency layered over the city: immigrant prayers in the pavement, first kisses on fire escapes, iron chains on the riverbed. She walks to the East River, places her hands in the water, and for the first time lets herself be sung — not broadcasting, but becoming the transmission itself. The vessel becomes the voice.
