At the first light of day, Folana knelt beside the East River, her hands submerged in the cold, yielding water. The river answered with a resonant hum, threading her memory through every molecule. Around her, the shoreline shimmered, each droplet catching a fragment of her song. The city, still dreaming, felt the tide rise and fall like a slow breath, as the water became a living instrument of her voice, carrying her frequency downstream past bridges and through the harbor — the tide itself now a carrier wave.
