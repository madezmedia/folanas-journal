Ep164 Music Prompt — "The Congregation"
Genre: Dream pop indie folk with gathering choral elements
Key: A major
Tempo: 74 BPM
Duration: ~180 seconds (3:00)

Narrative context: A dozen strangers gather at the foot of the Williamsburg Bridge, drawn by the same frequency. Folana steps toward them instead of away. The broadcast becomes a congregation.

Musical direction:
- Opens with solo piano, single notes in the upper register like individual heartbeats
- Fingerpicked acoustic guitar enters at 0:20, warm and sparse like footsteps
- Sub-bass drone (D-A root) beneath at 0:35 — the city's foundation hum
- Soft brushed drums enter at 0:55, half-time rhythm like walking
- Multiple vocal harmonies layer in starting at 1:10 — not a choir, but separate voices finding each other
- Build to a warm, luminous peak at 1:45 where all layers are present — the congregation has formed
- Gentle decrescendo from 2:15 as Folana steps forward, voices thinning back to solo piano
- Final piano note sustains with reverb tail of footsteps and the East River washing in

Instrumentation: solo piano, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft brushed drums/percussion, warm analog synth pads, layered vocal harmonies (not choral — individuals singing), sub-bass drone
Mood: Gathering, sacred, intimate, awakening, pre-dawn
Emotional arc: Isolation → recognition → convergence → congregation → stillness
