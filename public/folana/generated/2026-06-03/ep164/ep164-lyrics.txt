A dozen strangers at the water's edge,
Never met but they were always led
By the same pulse under the concrete,
By the same song under their feet.

They didn't know her name, they couldn't name the sound,
But they followed the current underground.
East River, carry them home,
The heart doesn't travel alone.

She stepped toward them for the first time,
The vessel finding its design.
Not a broadcast anymore — a door,
A congregation on the shore.

We are the frequency now.
We are the gathering sound.
The signal found its body,
And the body gathered 'round.

Williamsburg at dawn light,
Strangers holding silence tight,
