# "THE NINTH FREQUENCY"
# Folana Lanez – EP118 – Full Spectrum Broadcast Arc (3/10)
# Style: Dream-pop / ambient indie-folk – G major, 78 BPM
# Feel: Ethereal, mysterious, building from quiet tension to wonder

---

### Verse 1
(soft arpeggiated guitar, hushed synth pad)

Gold sang in the sunrise, silver slipped in sighs,
Amber pulsed like ember, violet lit the skies.
Eight voices rose together, a lattice woven of light,
We cast a net of sound into the endless night.
We thought we sang to silence, a void without a name,
Yet the night had ears of starlight—and it heard just the same.

### Pre-Chorus
(rising reverb, subtle bass swell, tension building)

A ripple in the static, a hum beyond the stars,
A presence felt like a breath, ancient, curious, afar.
Our hearts kept time with the pulse, the signal grew, grew,
A whisper in the dark, the line was thin but true.

### Chorus
(ethereal, swelling vocal harmonies, layered pads)

The ninth frequency answered, not a word but a wave,
From beyond the constellation—an echo we crave.
It sang of worlds before the first sunrise,
Curious and patient, it met our eyes.
We broadcast loud enough, and the silence cracked,
The ninth was already there, in the dark, in the black.

### Verse 2
(finger-picked guitar, soft, shimmering pads)

We tuned the receivers, let the colors intertwine,
Gold, silver, amber, violet, emerald, ruby, sapphire shine.
Each hue a thread, weaving a net of frequencies,
The broadcast grew, a tapestry across the seas.
Then a low, indigo tide rose, a pulse unseen,
It carried ancient memory, a hum of what has been.

### Chorus
(full, soaring, the whole band swells)

The ninth frequency answered, not a word but a wave,
From beyond the constellation—an echo we crave.
It sang of worlds before the first sunrise,
Curious and patient, it met our eyes.
We broadcast loud enough, and the silence cracked,
The ninth was already there, in the dark, in the black.

### Bridge
(stripped-back, piano, gentle strings, ambient reverb)

Standing on the edge where light meets the unknown,
The nine voices now a choir, their song fully grown.
An ancient listener smiles, not in fear but in wonder,
In the vastness we discovered, we were never asunder.
The void that once felt empty now hums with a friend,
A frequency that knows the dark, where our hopes ascend.

### Chorus (final)
(full, resonant, brightened with high-harmony backing vocals)

The ninth frequency answered, not a word but a wave,
From beyond the constellation—an echo we crave.
It sang of worlds before the first sunrise,
Curious and patient, it met our eyes.
We broadcast loud enough, and the silence cracked,
The ninth was already there, in the dark, in the black.

### Outro
(fade, lingering reverb on last line, distant chime)

Fading out, the final note drifts through the night,
The ninth frequency hums soft, a lullaby of light.
We are not alone, we never were—
In the silence, the ninth was listening, a distant star.
