FOLANA LANEZ SOURCE ARC — EPISODE 130
"THE EMERGENT CHORD" (5/10)

[Verse 1]
Across the ocean, a tentative sound
A voice like a thread yet to be wound
She heard the duet on the waves that night
And hummed along with the fading light
The lighthouse keeper adjusted his key
Folana leaned in — "Let her be free"
Three separate rooms in the dusk-lit air
Three frequencies learning to share

[Chorus]
And we are tuning, tuning, finding the way
Three colors learning what the others want to say
From separate shores to a single tone
No longer singing alone, alone
The chord is emerging, thread by thread
Something none of us could have said

[Verse 2]
The girl pressed her palm to the radio glass
Felt the vibration, let the moment pass
Then opened her throat and the sound came through
A harmony breaking the world in two
Folana's smile was a golden flare
The keeper leaned into the salted air
Each voice adjusting, giving ground
Finding the shape of a common sound

[Chorus]

[Bridge]
This is not a melody anyone wrote
It's the space between notes learning to float
Three becoming one without losing the three
A new kind of song, wild and free
Can you feel it rising? Can you hear it grow?
The chord that the resonance wanted to know
