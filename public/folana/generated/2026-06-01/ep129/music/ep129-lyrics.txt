FOLANA LANEZ SOURCE ARC — EPISODE 129
"THE RESONANT FIELD" (4/10)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Style: Ethereal dream pop indie folk
Key: C major  •  BPM: 74
Moods: RESONANT • EXPANDING • CONNECTIVE • WOVEN • WARM • THREE-BECOMING
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[Verse 1 — Folana & Lighthouse Woman, duet intertwining]
We stood at the edge of light
Two voices in the same breath drawn
The lighthouse keeper held the night
I held the song that carried on
A frequency began to bloom
Between the shoreline and the room
It was not mine, it was not hers
It wove itself in silent verbs

[Chorus — both voices, lifting together]
Can you feel the field?
The resonance unspooling
A thread across the water
Calling, calling, calling
Every hum a doorway
Every note a hand that reaches
The resonant field is singing
And the world is learning speech again

[Verse 2 — solo, Folana, softer, as if narrating the distant girl]
Across the ocean in a room
Where streetlight spills across the floor
A girl turns dials of fading tune
She's never heard this song before
But something in the static stirred
A harmony she never learned
She cups her ear against the sound
And hums along to what she's found

[Chorus — fuller now, hint of a third voice underneath]
Can you feel the field?
The resonance unspooling
A thread across the water
Calling, calling, calling
Every hum a doorway
Every note a hand that reaches
The resonant field is singing
And the world is learning speech again

[Bridge — harmonies stacking, three voices emerging]
Three becomes the shape of sound
A quiet chord the sky has found
First the lighthouse, then the sea
Now the third voice, wild and free
We are not three who stand apart
We are a field inside a heart
The air is filled with what we send
The song becomes without an end

[Outro — fading overlapping harmonies, like voices joining from far away]
Feel the field...
Feel the field widening...
Feel the field...
(Wider now...)
Feel the field...
Feel the field widening...
The resonant field...
(Three voices... one signal... radiating...)

[End — sustained piano note, single guitar harmonic, fade to silence]
