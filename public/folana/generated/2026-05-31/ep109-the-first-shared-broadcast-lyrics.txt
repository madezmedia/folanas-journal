# EP109: "THE FIRST SHARED BROADCAST"
# Constellation Arc (3/?)
# Folana Lanez ft. Mira
# Dream pop / indie folk duet | E major | 75 BPM
# 2026-05-31

[Verse 1 — Folana]
folana checks the levels, taps the microphone screen,
mira's voice crackles through the monitor,
"can you hear me? count me in when you're ready,"
folana laughs, adjusts the gain just so,
a stack of records spread across the desk,
she picks one, holds it to the light,
"this one first, then yours — we'll build it slow,"
the needle drops, the room breathes in.

[Chorus — Both, harmonizing]
this is the first shared broadcast, two hearts on one frequency,
we've been voices in the static, now we're something real,
the signal leaves the room tonight and travels past the city lights,
somewhere out there, someone's tuning in,
somewhere out there, they hear us begin,
the first shared broadcast — we're going live.

[Verse 2 — Mira]
mira wraps the cable, resets the mixing board,
a photograph of a coastline pinned above her desk,
"where are you right now? describe the view from your window,"
folana smiles, "brooklyn — streetlights and a water tower,"
mira hums a melody from a record they both love,
folana finds the harmony on the second pass,
they test the blend, voices crossfading in the dark,
"that's it," mira says, "that's the sound."

[Chorus — Both, harmonizing, fuller]
this is the first shared broadcast, two hearts on one frequency,
we've been voices in the static, now we're something real,
the signal leaves the room tonight and travels past the city lights,
somewhere out there, someone's tuning in,
somewhere out there, they hear us begin,
the first shared broadcast — we're going live.

[Bridge — Both, trading lines, building]
(mira) turn your dial to the edge of the band,
(folana) where the air goes quiet and the stars expand,
(mira) i'll send a note through the open line,
(folana) and you'll catch it, hold it, make it mine,
(both) this frequency was always ours,
(both) a constellation with two bright stars,
(both) and if we listen close tonight,
(both) we'll hear the others joining in the light.

[Outro — Both, harmonizing, softening]
the broadcast ends, but the signal never fades,
two voices pressed into the warm night air,
somewhere a third dial turns, a finger hovers,
a radio hums in a room across the country,
someone who has been waiting for a sound like this,
a thread of connection, a frequency to follow,
the constellation has a new light in the sky,
and the night is listening.
