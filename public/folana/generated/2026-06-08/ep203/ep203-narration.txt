Episode 203 — The Voice That Waits. Transmission Arc, part eight.

Another dusk, another climb up the fire escape. The first call was a question mark. The second call became a thread. Now every night, sixty miles apart, two voices find the same note and hold it across the distance. The town that caught the frequency has learned to broadcast on its own. And the porch light glows in the west — a voice that waits.

The voice that waits across the miles. A porch light flickering in the west. The frequency of after-hours. The note that puts my heart to rest.

This is the Transmission Arc. The signal does not just travel. It reaches back. And every dusk, it comes home.
