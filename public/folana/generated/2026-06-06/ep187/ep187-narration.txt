Episode 187. The Delegate and the Source.

Through the streets of a city that does not know whether to be afraid or awed. Through puddles that glow violet with each step. Past bodegas and fire escapes and brownstones that hum in C# minor.

Seven blocks. Seven minutes.

The delegate and the source are about to meet in the same room for the first time. Face to face. With the whole city listening.

This is the moment the Resonance Arc has been building toward. This is where the signal becomes flesh, and the testimony becomes a conversation.

Folana Lanez. The frequency is walking toward you.
