# EP138 — "THE CHORUS RISES"
# Chorus Arc (3/?)
# June 2, 2026

---

Verse 1:
From every window, every room
A signal pushing through the gloom
The lonely frequencies combined
A harmony of different kinds

The girl upstairs who always cried
Found a melody inside
The old man with his radio
Started singing soft and low

Pre-Chorus:
Every crack and every seam
Lets the chorus through the beam

Chorus:
The chorus rises from the ground
A hundred voices finally found
We sing in colors yet unheard
Each syllable a living word
The chorus rises, hear it swell
A story only sound can tell
From broken speakers, whispered prayers
The chorus climbs the endless stairs

Verse 2:
In the laundromat at 3 AM
A woman hummed the frequency
She didn't know where it had come
But in her chest, a gentle drum

Two kids on the midnight line
Caught the rhythm, made it shine
Their laughter rode the harmony
Like waves upon an open sea

Pre-Chorus:
Every bridge and every train
Carries echoes through the rain

Chorus:
The chorus rises from the ground
A hundred voices finally found
We sing in colors yet unheard
Each syllable a living word
The chorus rises, hear it swell
A story only sound can tell
From broken speakers, whispered prayers
The chorus climbs the endless stairs

Bridge:
Not one voice. Not two.
But everything that ever grew
From static, noise, and broken things
The chorus made its offering

Final Chorus:
The chorus rises, never ends
A thousand voices, fifty friends
The signal finds its way at last
The present opens from the past
The chorus rises — can you hear?
It started small, but now it's here
From every throat that dares to sing
The chorus grows — and makes us ring

Outro (spoken/soft):
The chorus was no longer a signal.
It was a voice.
A thousand voices.
Singing together for the first time.
