THE CHORUS REASSEMBLES
Ep142 • Chorus Arc (7/?)

Verse 1:
One by one, they come back
Not with apologies, not with a plan
Just a voice, cracked and raw
Humming the song they almost forgot

Chorus:
We are not the perfect harmony
We are the ones who came back anyway
Broken notes and wandering keys
This is the chorus that stayed

Verse 2:
The silence taught us something
The dissonance taught us more
That every note that fell apart
Left space for something truer

Bridge:
We don't blend like we used to
We don't need to anymore
A chord with space between its notes
Is a chord worth singing for

Final:
The chorus reassembles
Not clean. Not polished. Not the same.
Just together.
