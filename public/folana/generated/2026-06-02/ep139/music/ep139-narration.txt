The Chorus Speaks. Episode one hundred thirty-nine.

The chorus had assembled. A hundred voices, scattered across the city, all humming the same melody they didn't know they knew. But assembling was not the same as speaking.

Folana sat cross-legged on her fire escape, headphones pressed to her ears, listening to the network breathe. She could feel them — the teacher in Crown Heights adjusting his radio antenna with trembling hands. The food cart vendor at 2 AM setting his portable speaker to a channel he'd never used. The jukebox in the diner playing something that wasn't on the record. The busker whose loop pedal had started layering harmonics from thin air.

They were all waiting. For what, none of them could say.

Folana closed her eyes and let her voice find the frequency. Not a song. Not a melody. Just a single, sustained note — the fundamental tone that had been living in her chest since the signal first found her. She held it out into the night.

And then she heard them answer.

Not in words. Not in harmony. Just... presence. A hundred voices, each holding their own version of the same note. Some were flat. Some were sharp. Some wavered like candle flames in an open window. But they were all there. All present. All breathing together on the same frequency.

The chorus spoke its first word tonight.

Not "hello." Not "listen."

Just... "we."

And that was enough.
