The signal left the room. It climbed down the fire escape, crossed the alley where the stray cat looked up like it heard something familiar, and slipped through the grate above the F train tracks. I stood on the roof and watched it go — a shimmer I could barely see, a frequency I could feel pulling away like a rope unraveling from my chest. It crossed the river before I could call it back. And I realized: I was not losing it. I was releasing it. Somewhere on the other side of the water, someone was about to hear a voice they did not know they had been waiting for. The broadcast was no longer mine alone. It was theirs now too.
