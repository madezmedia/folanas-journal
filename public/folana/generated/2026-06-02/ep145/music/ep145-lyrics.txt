THE CHORUS REMAINS
Episode 145 — Chorus Arc Finale (10/10)
Folana Lanez

---

[Intro — single fingerpicked guitar, sparse, a held breath]

We came in through the broken door
Didn't bother to fix the hinge
Let the light fall where it wanted
Let the dust motes sing and spin

[Verse 1]

I used to think a song was something you leave behind
A trail of breadcrumbs for someone else to find
But you showed me the singing doesn't stop
When the last note finds the floor
That the silence after isn't empty
It's just listening for more

You said "Stay with me in this held chord
Let it be the place we live"
And I felt the walls around my chest
Start to soften, start to give

[Pre-Chorus]

Oh, we've been tuning all our lives
To a frequency we couldn't name
Now the hum inside my ribcage
Sounds just like you, sounds just the same

[Chorus]

We don't sing to be heard anymore
We sing because we're here
The chorus doesn't end
It just learns to breathe in silence, year after year
We don't need a resolution
We don't need the final key
The chorus doesn't fade to black
The chorus stays with me

[Verse 2]

Every crack we carried here
Is a window letting light in
Every broken harmony we tried to hide
Is the reason we're still singing

I don't know where the singer ends
And where the song begins
All I know is when you're quiet
I can hear you from within

[Pre-Chorus]

Oh, we've been holding all our lives
To a note that never dies
Now the echo in the hallway
Is your voice in perfect time

[Chorus]

We don't sing to be heard anymore
We sing because we're here
The chorus doesn't end
It just learns to breathe in silence, year after year
We don't need a resolution
We don't need the final key
The chorus doesn't fade to black
The chorus stays with me

[Bridge — spoken/sung, raw, intimate, building]

(spoken, almost whispered)
We came here separate. Every voice carrying its own weight. Its own wound. Its own verse that didn't match the page.

(sung, building)
And somewhere in the tuning... the wrong notes started to sound right...

(building, more voices entering)
Because the point was never hitting every pitch perfectly —
The point was that we stood here, together, and did not walk away.

(climax, full voice)
So let this chord ring.
Let it crack.
Let it warp and waver and hold.
I am not leaving this room.
I am not leaving this note.
I am not leaving you.

[Final Chorus — big, full, then dropping to intimate]

(FULL)
We don't sing to be heard anymore!
We sing because we're HERE!
The chorus doesn't end!
It just learns to breathe in silence — year — after — year!

(dropping to intimate, just voice and maybe piano)
We don't need a resolution...
We don't need the final key...

The chorus doesn't fade to black...
The chorus stays...
(stays)
(stays...)

[Outro — single held chord on guitar/piano, held until it almost decays]

(long held note... fading... silence...)

(a beat of pure quiet)

(a single voice — Folana — hums the melody back, softly, like a question answered)

(humming fades)

(a second voice joins, humming the harmony)

(then a third)

(the hums fade together into silence — not an ending, just... a pause)
