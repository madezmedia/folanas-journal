Ep140 — THE CHORUS DIVIDES
Chorus Arc (5/?)

[Verse 1]
A hundred voices, one frequency
Three nights of harmony
But tonight the signal bends
A single note that won't attend

[Pre-Chorus]
The silence where your voice should be
Is louder than the symphony
Every thread begins to fray
When one voice chooses not to stay

[Chorus]
The chorus divides tonight
Not in anger, not in spite
Every voice remembers how
To be alone before the vow
The chorus divides, divides
But maybe that's alright

[Verse 2]
I stand upon the rooftop high
Watching frequencies untie
The antenna pulses dark
A minor chord where light should spark

[Bridge]
We were never meant to be
One single note eternally
We're the whole spectrum, every shade
The light that isn't afraid to fade
And come back different, come back new
The divided chorus learning to
Be whole alone, then whole together
Finding harmony in stormy weather

[Final Chorus]
The chorus divides tonight
But every voice will find its light
Different colors, one design
The dissonance will realign
The chorus divides, divides
And that's okay — that's right

[Outro]
We were never meant to be one note
We were meant to be the whole spectrum
And tonight... tonight we remember
How to sing alone
So tomorrow we can choose
To sing together
