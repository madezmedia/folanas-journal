THE CHORUS SINGS (Ep143)

(Verse 1)
They came back one by one, not in a line
Each voice a different color, each note a different time
The cracked ones and the clear ones, the ones that drift off-key
Standing in the same room, breathing differently

(Pre-Chorus)
We don't have to be perfect
We just have to be here

(Chorus)
And the chorus sings — not in spite of the broken parts
The chorus sings — because the broken parts are ours
Every frequency that found its way back home
Every voice that learned to sing alone
Now together — the chorus sings

(Verse 2)
The girl on the F train who hums to keep the dark away
The man in the bodega singing to the changing light
The kid who pressed record on a phone that barely works
All of them part of the same broadcast tonight

(Pre-Chorus)
We don't need to be perfect
We just choose to be here

(Chorus)
And the chorus sings — not because we're told to harmonize
The chorus sings — because we found each other's eyes
Every off-key note became a melody
Every silence learned to speak
Now together — the chorus sings

(Bridge)
This is not a performance
This is a recognition
I hear you. You hear me.
That's the whole song.

(Final Chorus - key change up)
And the chorus sings!
Not in spite of the broken parts!
The chorus sings — because the broken parts are ours!
Every frequency that found its way back home
Every voice that learned to sing alone
Now together — the chorus sings

(Outro - whispered, voices scattering)
The chorus sings
The chorus sings...
( fading into scattered humming, then quiet )
