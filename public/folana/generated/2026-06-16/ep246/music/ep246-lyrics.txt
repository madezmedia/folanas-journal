THE CROWN EXPANDS — EP246

[Verse 1]
The sycamore stood at dawn
New green at every tip
I traced the branch I knew so well
To where the crown begins to slip
Into the open air above
Where light pools thick and warm
The tender shoots have not yet hardened
Against the coming storm

[Chorus]
The crown expands — not to be seen
Because the light is there
The crown expands — not to be whole
Because the breath is air
Every leaf a tiny hand
Reaching past the last
The crown does not ask where it's going
It grows because it must

[Verse 2]
I pressed my palm against the bark
And felt the movement rise
The same flow from the root below
That fed the leaves' surprise
Now feeding edges of the crown
Where I have never been
The tree becomes what it is reaching for
And I become what I have seen
