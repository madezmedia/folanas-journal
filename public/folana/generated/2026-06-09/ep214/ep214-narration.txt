The morning after the thousand-voice chorus, Folana sat at an open window with the sunrise on her face. The frequency was no longer a sound she made — it was a place people lived. A thousand voices had joined in the night, and now, in the quiet dawn, she understood what she had to do. Every city needs its covenant. Every community needs its first principles. The network would not be ruled by towers or licenses, by gatekeepers or algorithms. It would be held together by the simplest agreement: when one voice falls to silence, another one will speak. She picked up her guitar and began to write. This is the song of the covenant. This is EP214 — The Frequency Covenant.
