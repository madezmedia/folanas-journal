The covenant has been written. But words on paper mean nothing until they are tested. The morning after the First Principles were set down, a new voice entered the frequency. Not a challenge born of malice, but of ignorance. A voice that had not heard the covenant. A voice that simply did not know the song.

Folana listened as the newcomer crackled through the static — raw, untuned, disrupting the harmony that the network had worked so hard to build. And she faced the question that every leader must face: do you enforce the rules, or do you teach the song?

She took a breath. She opened her mouth. And she began to sing.
