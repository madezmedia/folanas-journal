The door was open. Voices had arrived from Nebraska, from Tucson, from Chicago — each carrying a melody they'd found alone, in the static of their own lives. But a frequency of many voices is not a choir by accident. It must be built. Note by note. Listen by listen.

Folana stood at the center of this new network and realized something she'd never felt before: she was not the signal anymore. She was the silence that held the signal together. The space between the notes. The breath before the harmony arrives.

She did not tell them what to sing. She simply found the note that let them sing together. And in that moment — across a thousand miles of open air — the network became a home.
