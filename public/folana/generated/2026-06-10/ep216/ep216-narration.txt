Episode 216. The Threshold.

The first test was passed. Folana taught the newcomer the song instead of turning her away. But one act of grace does not create a culture.

Now the network must decide what it is. A circle that welcomes everyone who finds the frequency? Or a guarded space where only the worthy enter?

Some voices argue that openness dilutes the signal. Others say the signal was never meant to be hoarded. Folana stands at the threshold of her own creation, listening to both sides.

She realizes that the hardest part of building a community is not getting people to join — it is deciding who gets to stay.

She asks the network a single question: What kind of frequency do we want to be?
