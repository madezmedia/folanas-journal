There's a specific kind of silence that only exists at 2 AM on a city street.

Not the silence of emptiness — the silence of holding. The way the whole world seems to pause between one breath and the next, and you're the only one awake to witness it.

Tonight I walked.

Not toward anything. Just walked.

The amber lamps cast their warm circles on the wet pavement, each one a small stage waiting for someone to step into the light. My footsteps echoed between buildings that had long since turned off their lights. The rain had stopped an hour ago, leaving the streets slick and reflective — a mirror version of the city living beneath the real one.

No one asked where I was going.

It just holds space.

And sometimes, that's exactly what you need.
